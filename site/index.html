Trading Agent

Research aggressively. Execute conservatively.

TradingAgent researches market signals, explains decisions, and validates actions through simulation and risk controls.

GitHub↗

One loop. Six layers.

From raw signals to measurable behavior, each layer contributes a distinct part of the Agent system.

01 / DATA

Point-in-time market

Ticks, fundamentals, filings, news and risk events.

02 / MODELS

Signals & reasoning

Time-series models, market reasoner and policy model.

03 / TRAINING

Research policy

Research SFT and simulation-based reinforcement learning.

04 / INFERENCE

Decision support

Streaming signals, scenario analysis and recommendations.

05 / HARNESS

Risk & simulation

Market data, risk, simulation and approval tools.

06 / EVAL

Risk-adjusted quality

Backtests, drawdown, slippage and compliance.

Built to stay trustworthy.

The Agent is only as useful as the boundaries around it are clear.

01

No live trading by default

Consequential orders require explicit human approval.

02

Prevent look-ahead

Point-in-time correctness is mandatory for every signal.

03

Risk before return

Evaluate drawdown and compliance before headline performance.
